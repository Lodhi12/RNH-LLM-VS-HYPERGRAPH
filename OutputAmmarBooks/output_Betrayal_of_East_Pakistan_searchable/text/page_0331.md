Discipline in the Field
I have written a couple of letters to you and J hope that you have
passed the contents to all offrs under your command. 
I 
realize
that it may be a little too early to see the results. However, it is
disturbing for me to see elementary mistakes being committed in
the field. These need to be checked and corrected. If comds and
offrs gloss over them now, we would only be encouraging offrs
and men to get into bad habits to the detriment of battle
efficiency. | would, therefore, like comds at all levels to pay special
attention to details and matters concerning battle or field
discipline.
During my recent visits to some of the units in fwd areas, I got
the impression that there is an obvious lack of battle discipline;
