Kushtia-Khuina sector. There are two major roads in the area, one
from Kushtia to Khulna and the other from Benapol, on the
Indian border, to Jessore-Jhenida-Faridpur. This area is
interspersed with many small rivers, and cross-country movement
is difficult. Jessore provides a good airfield, and at Chalna a
seaport has been developed.
Sylhet-Comilla-Chittagong sector. Chittagong is a seaport. This sector
is separated by the Meghna River from Dhaka. There is ome
direct road to Dhaka. There is only one railway bridge, at Bhairab
Bazar, connecting Dhaka with the eastern part of the province.
At places the railway line runs just along the border with Agartala.
There are only two major roads from south to north, Sylhet to
Chittagong and Dhaka to Chittagong. There are numerous rivers
en route making movement slow and difficult. Chittagong has a
good airfield but only for small aircraft.
Dhaka sector. It is the centre of political and military gravity. This

over of forces from one sector to the other was not possible due
