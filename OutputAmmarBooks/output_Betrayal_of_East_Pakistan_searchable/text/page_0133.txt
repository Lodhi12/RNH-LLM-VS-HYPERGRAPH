After the briefing, and as per GHQ’s requirement, Chandpur
was to be occupied, which I had left to elements from 14 Division
which were adequate for the task. However, I raised 39 ad hoc
Division under Major-General M. Rahim Khan, HJ, with the staff
to be drawn from HQ Martial Law and with communications
from HQ 53 Brigade, which was also moved to area Chodagram-
Laxam. The move of 53 Brigade, which was Command Reserve
and whose priority task was that of defence of Dhaka, was done
in view of GHQ’s promises of eight additional battalions. With
the move of 53 Brigade no regular formation was left in Dhaka
ull I pulled back some troops from the divisions. The two
additional battalions which arrived were sent to 9 Division 
‘to
make up the deficiency in Jessore Sector, and the remaining six
were planned to be deployed in Dhaka; HQ 39 Division was
established on an island near Chandpur, where civil
communications were available. With the induction of 39
Division, the area of responsibility of 14 Division was now changed
and the two divisions were responsible as follows: 14 Division
(seven battalions) from Sylhet to Kasba including River Meghna;
39 Division from and excluding Kasba to Feni (four battalions).
Lt.-Gen. Arora, GOC Eastern Command, had deployed three
corps and a communication zone round East Pakistan. The corps
under Lt.-Gen. Sagat Singh was deployed opposite Sylhet,
Chittagong Sector; 101 communication zone under Maj-Gen.
G. S. Gill was deployed against Mymensingh Sector; 33 Corps
under Lt.-Gen. Thapan was deployed opposite Rajshahi Sector;
and If Corps under Lt.-Gen. Raina was deployed against Jessore
sector.
