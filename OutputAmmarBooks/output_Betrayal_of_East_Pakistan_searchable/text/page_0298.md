RETURN TO PAKISTAN 
251
After ten days’ leave, I was called to GHQ in Rawalpindi to
make my report there, and to write my report for the Hamood-
ur-Rehman Commission. GHQ gave me the cold shoulder and I
did not get any help or encouragement from there. I asked
General Tikka to give me the correspondence exchanged
between Governor Malik and the President and between my HO
and GHQ. He was unable to do so as it was all with Mr Bhutto. I
also applied to Chief Justice Hamood-ur-Rehman himself, who
was likewise unable to get the documents from Bhutto. We had
destroyed our records when the orders for surrender were
received. GHQ provided all facilities and all our correspondence
to Major-General Mugeem for his book on the 1971 crisis, but I
was refused help,in completing my reports in spite of the fact
that I was still in uniform. Mr Muzaffar Hussain, Chief Secretary
East Pakistan, gave me copies of some of the signals between
myself and GHQ, and between the Governor and the President.
This helped me a lot in completing my reports. It was a very
noble gesture on his part.
