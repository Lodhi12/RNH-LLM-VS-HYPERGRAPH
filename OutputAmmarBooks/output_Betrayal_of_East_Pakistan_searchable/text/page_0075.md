We had deficiencies, problems, and obstacles, but we had
certain advantages. I had vast experience of commanding troops. |
The troops under my command were probably the best in the
world. We were a regular army and not a conscripted one and
had military traditions and a fighting background. Most of the
units under my command had fought all the battles and wars of
Pakistan and quite a few had taken part in World War II. My
senior officers, particularly the generals, had fought all the wars
of Pakistan and taken part in World War II as well. They had vast
experience of command of troops in both peace and war. I knew
all my officers from Lt.-Col. and above, and many [COs and
senior NCOs.
