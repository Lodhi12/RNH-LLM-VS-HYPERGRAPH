Another pertinent question arises: if Bhutto did not want us
and Mrs Gandhi was willing to keep us, then how did we come
back? Although Bangladesh was established, until it was
recognized by Pakistan it had no status. So the Second Islamic

Summit was exploited and cleverly used as a ruse by Bhutto to
recognize the new State. The second stage was its becoming a
member of the UN. When the time for this came, ‘China
threatened to veto...the entry of Bangladesh to the UN and thus
enabled Pakistan to secure the release of prisoners of war.’ (Dawn,
Karachi, 27 December 1993) So India had perforce to send us,
and Bhutto had to accept us.
