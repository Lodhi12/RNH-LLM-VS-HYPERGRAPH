Pakistan Army – The Instrument of Change 
Present political class ruling the country is not likely to bring about essential 
reforms that disturb status quo. They are beneficiaries of corrupt system that 
keeps the masses poor, helpless and downtrodden.   
Change along the lines suggested above can come if Pakistan Army takes up the 
challenge. Army High Command should work out a comprehensive plan and 
implementing methodology. This should be presented to the Prime Minster and 
his cabinet. If the Prime Minister and his government are unwilling to carry out 
reforms, then the President of Pakistan should be asked to impose emergency, 
dissolve parliament, remove governments and initiate the process of changes 
with full backing of the Army. The people are most likely to welcome and support 
the changes to get free from the strangulating grip of corrupt mafias ruling the 
country. 
Taking bold steps to carry out basic reforms will enable our country to recover 
from downwards slide it has been put into during the last so many years.
