A major operation against TPP was carried out during 2009 when the terrorists 
briefly established their rule in Malakand Division threatening occupation of 
Islamabad. Pakistan armed forces dislodged the terrorists entrenched on vast 
mountainous areas in a swift, wisely planned and audaciously executed operation 
that dashed all hopes and expectations of our enemies. Terrorist’s dens housing 
‘Human Bomb Factories’ mainly in Mehsud tribes areas of South Waziristan were 
also dismantled during 2009. The modern day followers of ‘Hasan bin Sabah’, like 
Qari Hussain Mahsud and others involved in preparing teen age boys as suicide 
bombers to explode against selected targets had to close shop.  
Pakistan has evolved appropriate response to deal with this serious threat using 
all elements of State power after the tragic incident of Army Public School 
Peshawar where about one hundred and fifty children and their teachers were 
brutally murdered on 16 December 2014. Before that it was mainly left to the law 
enforcing agencies (LEAs) to deal with threat, the forces deployed on road check 
posts waiting for attacks by terrorists, the initiative with them rather than being 
with the State of Pakistan. Operation ‘Zarb e Azb’ started in 2014, being 
conducted in all parts of Pakistan to free the country from menace of all kinds of 
terrorism is progressing steadily to achieve the desired targets.
