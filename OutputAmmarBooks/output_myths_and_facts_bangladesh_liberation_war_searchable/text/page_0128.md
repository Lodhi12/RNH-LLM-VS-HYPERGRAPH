Nixon concurred with Kissinger’s observation that “we have to continue to squeeze the Indians, even when this thing is settled.” He instructed
that the economic aid program for India be reprogrammed to help pay for the war damage suffered by Pakistan. Nixon also angrily instructed that
a concerted effort be made to publicize India’s role in the crisis: “Get a white paper out. I want the Indians blamed for this, you know what I
mean? We can’t let these goddamn sanctimonious Indians get away with this. Here they are raping and murdering. They talk about West Pakistan.
These Indians are pretty vicious.”
