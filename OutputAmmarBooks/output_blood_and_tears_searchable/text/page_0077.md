Many other parts of the Chittagong Division were stricken by the Awami
League-instigated civil strife in March and April 1971. Witnesses said that the
slaying of non-Bengalis and the wanton looting of their property had taken place
in Nazirhat, Anwara, Dohazari, Kumira and Hathazari. When tension was
sparked off at these places in the middle of March, 1971, many non-Bengali
families fled to Chittagong city. In their absence, vandals looted their houses. A
few non-Bengali families in Cox’s Bazar were also the victims of genocidal
violence.
