“In the second week of March 1971, some non-Bengalis were roughed up,
without the slightest provocation, by Awami League militants. On March 20,
1971, when I went to my office, some Bengali colleagues warned me that the
Awami League militants would attack the houses of non-Bengalis. They urged
me not to go back to my apartment. In the afternoon, armed bands of Awami
Leaguers went on the rampage against non-Bengalis, looting their houses and
gunning the men to death. I hid myself for three days in the bathroom of my
office building. Subsequently, I sought refuge in the home of a Bengali colleague.
The killers had raided the communal apartment where I used to live and they
had murdered.
