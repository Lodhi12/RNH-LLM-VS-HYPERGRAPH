Battle of Jessore
The Jessore sector in the south-west corner of East Pakistan
extended from a line joining the towns of Jibannagar,
Kotchandpur and Kaliganj in the north to the dense forests of the
Sunderbans on the shores of the Bay of Bengal in the south. A
frontage of nearly 112 kilometres. To the east flowed the mighty
Ganges. Just across the western border with India lay the
metropolis of Calcutta - a city of teaming millions most of whose
inhabitants slept in the open. A credible threat in that direction
would have caused considerable panic amongst its civilian
population. General Dalbir Singh, confided to Brigadier
Muhammad Hayat, after the war, that if the Pakistani troops had
even advanced three kilometres towards Calcutta the people
would have fled the city.
