At the time of Partition only one brigade was stationed in Ras,
Bengal out of a total strength of four infantry divisions. Late,
when the Pakistan army expanded, the force in East Pakistan Was
raised to one infantry division.
After the 1962 Indo-Chinese conflict the Indians began ty
strengthen their armed forces even further. They began to pay
greater attention towards their eastern and northeastern flank. A
second Indian fleet came up in the Bay of Bengal. A ring of air
bases was constructed around Pakistan’s eastern province. The
infrastructure close to the international frontier in the east began
to be developed. India began to be concerned about the
vulnerability of their chicken neck connecting West Bengal with
Assam. They were bitter against Islamabad for closing the more
direct land link which had passed through East Pakistan.
Even during the 1965 Indo-Pakistan War no need was felt to
bolster up the defence of East Pakistan as India had made no
military moves in that direction. The Bengali politicians, however,
| capitalised on the lack of sensitivity shown by the Centre towards
the security of their province.
