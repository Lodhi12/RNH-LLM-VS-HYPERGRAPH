As the date for the Indian attack came near the rebellious Units
of the EBR, along with Indian army per sonnel, tried to capture

evacuated to Indian medical centres where they were given
medical treatment and the sick and wounded were looked after.
By mid-November highly exaggerated reports, for the
consumption of the international media, of the achievement of the
Mukti Bahini, started coming in. Over 25,000 regular soldiers of
Pakistan were reported killed*!, They claimed that out of five
divisions atleast three had been put out of commission by
November 21. Not even ten percent of what was being put across
was, however, true. The effectiveness of the Mukti Bahini was
grossly inflated to build up their own morale, lower that of the
Pakistan army and gain more international support.
