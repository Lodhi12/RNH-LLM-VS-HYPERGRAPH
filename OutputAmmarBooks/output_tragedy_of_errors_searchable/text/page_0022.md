Fazlul Haq continued working for the Muslim League and
denounced the All India Congress calling it "satan incarnate" as
according to him it was creating divisions among the Muslim
ranks*. He was, however, not quite happy with the kind of
discipline Jinnah had imposed on the members of the Muslim
League. Fazlul Haq was not willing to give up his style of taking
independent decisions concerning his own future and that of the
Bengalis. To him Bengal came first for he was primarily a Bengali
at heart and placed his countrymen of all castes and creeds above
all else. He met and talked to Abul Kalam Azad knowing full well
that Mr. Jinnah did not like Azad being accepted as a leader of the
Muslims of India. Though Fazlul Haq met Azad occasionally he
was unhappy with the actions of the Congress dominated
governments.
