Operational Policy
The political directive to Eastern Command till then was not to
allow the Mukti Bahini to control any territory in East Pakistan
on which they could declare a government of Bangladesh. What
haunted Niazi was the fear that the Mukti Bahini would do just
that. He was determined not to allow the secessionists to capture
even a small portion of East Pakistan territory. He, therefore,
locked his mind on this one goal alone. He was certain that he
would be able to prevent rebels from their nefarious designs. The
fact that India would invade East Pakistan with full force was not
yet visualised by him.
