Indian Plan in the South-east Sector
Indian 23 Mountain Division was given the task to close up to
the River Meghna as soon as possible and simultaneously cut the
road and rail network at Feni thus preventing the two brigades in
the Chittagong hill tracts from reinforcing the main defences
around Laksham. Comilla was to be by-passed and the road to
Chandpur from Laksham blocked before the units on the front
line could fall back.
