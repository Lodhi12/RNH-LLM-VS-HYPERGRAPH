Political Quagmire
(Beginning of the End)
On January 29, 1968 Field Marshal Ayub suffered a severe
heart attack and remained seriously ill for about 10 days which
kept him out of his office for a month and a half. According to the
constitution the speaker of the national assembly should have
been handed over power during the period of his illness. But that
was not done as Ayub never trusted him. This deliberate violation
_of the constitutional requirement angered the Bengalis. In July
the president went to England for a medical check up. He once
again departed from the democratic practice by keeping Mr. Abdul
Jabbar Khan away from the presidential chair. This encouraged
his opponents to increase the pressure on him to quit his office.
In West Pakistan, Zulfiqar Ali Bhutto’s agitation against the
_ Field Marshal began to gain ground. Students, teachers, lawyers
and the men in the street joined him in his tirade against the
president. On November 7, 1968, the students of the polytechnic
School just outside Rawalpindi clashed with the police. One
student by the name of Abdul Hameed, was killed. Bhutto,
chairman of the newly formed Pakistan People’s Party, took
advantage of this and turned the funeral procession into a political .
‘'\gathering. From then on demonstrations, strikes, gheraos
(besiegement), burning, looting, lathi charges, brickbatting
became almost daily occurrences. Curfew was challenged, army
Was frequently called out. On November 11, one Hashim, an
unknown figure, fired a pistol at the president during his visit to
