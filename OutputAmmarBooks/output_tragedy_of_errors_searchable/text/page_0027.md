Tragedy of Errors
On March 6, 1948 Suharwardy made his first speech in the
Constituent Assembly of Pakistan, while still being a citizen of
India. In his speech he advocated compassion for the minorities...
"Take within your fold the non-Muslim minority _ Said
Suharwardy. Since he had not yet become a resident of Pakistan,
his name was not included in the central cabinet from East
- Bengal: Fazlur Rahman’s name was sent instead. A resolution
was passed saying that no one, who would not take up residence
in Pakistan within six months of the passing of this resolution,
would continue to be a member of the Constituent Assembly‘, As
he had not taken up permanent residence in Pakistan till March
1949, much after the expiry of the six months period allowed to
change one’s nationality his seat was officially declared vacant. He
eventually came to Pakistan in 1950 and started practising law.
Even then the courts in Lahore and Karachi were told not to
register him as a lawyer”.
The Awami League
As the partition of India approached a reality, differences
began to emerge amongst the old Muslim Leaguers of Bengal. The
Suharwardy-Hashim.Group did not see eye to eye with the
Hassan Manzil Group of Khwaja Nazimuddin. To obtain a
platform to air their views the former had formed the Gana Azadi
League in Dacca in July, 1947. Two years after independence the
split had widened with each going its own way. On June 23, 1949
a number of Muslim League members of the provincial legislature
held a convention at Dacca and founded the East Pakistan Awami
Muslim League”, under the chairmanship of Maulana Abdul
_, Hameed Bhashani. It had a 42 point manifesto which included
"among other things: Bengali to be the state language and
maximum regional autonomy for the provinces with only three
subjects with the Centre viz:- defence, foreign affairs and
currency?’,
Nine months later Suharwardy laid the foundation of his own
political party which he called the Pakistan Awami League. It
became the first ppposition party in Pakistan. The first meeting of
the Pakistan Awami League was held in Lahore on March 19 and
20, 1950 at Mochi Gate. At this meeting Maulana Bhashani was
elected as the president of the party. Ata-ur-Rahman, Abdul
Mansoor Ahmad and Abdus Salam were chosen to be its vice
