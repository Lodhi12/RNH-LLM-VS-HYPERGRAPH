564 Tragedy of Errors
The area around Hilli was plain and dry in the month of
November. No major water obstacles existed to break the
momentum of the attack. Everything seemed tailor made for the
attackers, but yet the brigade defending Hilli withstood the
onslaught till the very end.
205 Brigade was commanded by Brigadier Tajammul Hussein
Malik who had earned his spurs at Batapur, (West Pakistan), as
the commanding officer of 3 Baloch, in September 1965, when he,
alongwith others, had stopped the advance of the Indian army
towards Lahore. This time his task was to prevent them from
reaching Bogra.
Tajammul was a product of Chakwal in the martial belt of
undivided India. He was commissioned in the 7th Rajput
Regiment in February 1947. He was known in the army for his
doggedness and his determination to pursue a course of action,
which he perceived to be correct, to the limit, irrespective of its
consequences. A streak of religious fanaticism made him
unmindful of his personal safety. It also gave him perseverance in
adversity. It was this trait in his character which made him insist
eo every inch of the area assigned to him would be defended and
at there would be no withdrawal to the designated fortress of
Bogra. (a correct attitude at the company and battalion level but
not necessarily so when fighting a corps or theatre battle).
