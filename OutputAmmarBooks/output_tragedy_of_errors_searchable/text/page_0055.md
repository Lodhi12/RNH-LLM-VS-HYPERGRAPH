After Liagat Ali Khan’s death the Muslim League split into a
Bengali faction headed by Khwaja Nazimuddin and a Punjabi
faction supported by Governor General Ghulam Muhammad.
Quite expectedly when Nazimuddin drafted another BPC Report
which gave an equal number of seats both in the lower and upper
house of the parliament to both the wings it was rejected this time
by the West Pakistani political leaders. A third draft presented by
Muhammad Ali Bogra, from East Bengal, was also considered
unsuitable by the politicians in West Pakistan as they feared
domination of the Bengalis over them, as some West Pakistani
members could always be ‘persuaded’ to join the East Bengalis on
issues which would be advantageous to their province.
