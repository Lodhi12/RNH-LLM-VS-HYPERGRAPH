My thanks are also due to the numerous military officers of
various ranks who agreed to give me the details of the war in the
Eastern Theatre.
Mr. Mahmood Ali, Khwaja Khairuddin, Professor Abdul
Qayyoom, Major General Khalil-ur-Rahman, shared with me
impressions of their ‘Sonar Bangla’ which they had left behind.
The Bangladesh Institute of International and Strategic Studies
were over generous in arranging my visit to Dhaka and allowing
me access to the various literature available in its library. It
helped me in getting interviews with many distinguished
personalities who played a role during their independence
movement. I am greatly indebted to Chairman, Mr. Murshid and
the renowned director general of the BIISS, Brigadier (retired)
Abdul Hafeez for the assistance they provided.
Pakistan’s High Commissioner in New Delhi, Abdus Sattar
