Consequent to the operational directive received from GHQ, 14
Division issued the following operational instruction on 17
September, 1970 based on a threat estimate of approximately
three infantry divisions.
Operational Instructions of 14 Division,
17 September, 1970
The Mission specified was:
1) Defend East Pakistan while inflicting maximum attrition
on the enemy but avoiding the destruction of own forces
and;
2) Ensure defence of Dacca at all costs.
The Plan to accomplish the mission was:
1) Troops should be prepared to fight a series of delaying
actions.
2) While withdrawing to the main defences, leave stay behind
parties to carry out harassment of the enemy during their
advance.
3) Self contained sectors to be designated (later further
divided into fortresses and strong points).
4) Small scale tactical offensive operations to be undertaken.
5) No change in the basic assumptions.
