The main issues which the government of Yahya Khan faced
after the elections were: (1) should power be handed over to the
majority party without further delay or negotiations? (2) should
consensus on a new constitution be obtained inside or outside the *
Assembly? (3) should martial law be lifted before holding the —
national assembly session or both should be done simultaneously?
(4) should the constitution be framed at a joint session of the two
major parties or each party be allowed to put up their own drafts
and then the government steps in to merge them and achieve a
consensus? (5) should there be a power sharing between the |
Awami League and the Pakistan People’s Party or this be left to
the majority party to form its own coalition partners and (6)
should the time limit of 120 days be removed?
