In India the West Bengalis are very different to the East

not the same. the Madrasis differ considerably from the Hindus of
the United Provinces in race, colour, language, customs but yet

become a cause for the separation of the two wings of Pakistan?
None of the demographic dissimilarities would have altered the
loyalty of the Bengalis towards Pakistan if they had not been

The language riots had already resulted in a bitter feeling
against the Centre. Emotions were again aroused when Governor
Abdul Monem Khan of East Pakistan and Khwaja Shahabuddin,
the minister of information and broadcasting, himself a Bengali,
but from the urban elite, banned the songs of Rabinder Nath
Tagore from being broadcast from Radio Dacca and other radio
stations in East Pakistan. A very unwise step indeed as by then
Bengali had been accepted as the official language of Pakistan. °
Tagore (1861-1941), was born in Kushtia, East Bengal, which
added to his credentials in the eyes of the East Bengalis. He
occupied a place of pride in Bengali literature. The Bengalis must
have tuned in to Calcutta to listen to his poems which was what
Pakistani officials wanted to avoid. His poems could have aroused
feelings of Bengali nationalism but so did the writings of Khushal
Khan Khattak in the Frontier and his poetry was not banned in
West Pakistan. I do not agree with Maswani that "an attempt was
