The correspondence between Mr. Jinnah and Mahatam,
Gandhi of September, 1944 also clearly brings out the thinking of
the founder of the nation on the question of the creation of one or
more Muslim states after the independence of India. Toa question
posed by the Hindu leader that “are the constituents in the two

in each zone", the Quaid replied “No they will form units of
Pakistan". Again replying to Gandhi’s query about the provision of
defence Mr. Jinnah replied "There cannot be defence and similar
matters of common concern when it is accepted that Pakistan and
Hindustan will be two separate independent states"
