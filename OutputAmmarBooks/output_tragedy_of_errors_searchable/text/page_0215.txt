East Pakistan Rifles (EPR)
The East Pakistan Rifles was formed to guard the border
between India and East Bengal in peace time and to reinforce the
Pakistan army in defending its territorial integrity and national
independence in war. The border was divided into the following
six sectors: Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Dinajpur, Rajshahi, and
Jessore. Each sector was composed of six wings of 450 men each.
Most of the men were Bengalis while their officers came both
: from East and West Pakistan. Mymensing had an independent
wing.
