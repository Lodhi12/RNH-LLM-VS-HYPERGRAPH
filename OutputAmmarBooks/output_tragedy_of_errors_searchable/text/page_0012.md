This is the result of nearly three years of hard labour. It is
based on a deep study of documents, newspapers, articles, books
and personal interviews of those who were then at the helm of
affairs. The book focuses on the geo-strategic implication of a
country divided by a thousand miles of enemy territory. It brings
out the causes of the progressive alienation of the Bengalis from
West Pakistan. It highlights the political bitnglings of the civil and
military leadership during the first twenty-four years of Pakistan’s
existence. It analyses the war plans of India and Pakistan in 1971.
It focuses attention on the yole of India in this sordid drama and
finally it looks into the impact of the dismemberment of Pakistan
on India, Pakistan and Bangladesh. :
