west of these crags Kashmir is green and lush from heavy annual rainfall.  Though not so high as Ladakh, 
the thickly wooded slopes of Kashmir are rugged and pose a significant obstacle to military movement. 
Furthermore, in 1971, neither side planned major offensive operations in the Tangdhar and Uri sectors.
