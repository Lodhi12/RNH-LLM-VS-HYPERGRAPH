Bangladesh: Northwest Sector
Somewhat drier than the other operational areas in East Pakistan, the northwest sector offered 
better terrain for mechanized forces and the possibility of fairly rapid movement.  Consequently, 
both sides allotted the bulk of their armored units to this sector (Pakistani 29 Cavalry; Indian 63 and 
69 Armored Regiments, as well as 20 Maratha Light Infantry in SKOT armored personnel carriers)
