Other Bangladeshi groups constituted “Freedom Fighters” who operated deep within East 
Pakistan as guerrillas and political cadres.  Although the Freedom Fighters seldom inflicted heavy 
losses on the Pakistani regulars, they represented a ubiquitous menace, constantly harassing 
their opponents with ambushes, raids, sabotage, and propaganda.  Their activities exhausted the 
Pakistani troops while creating an enervating sense of constant uncertainty and danger.  They also 
supplied Indian authorities with invaluable intelligence information.  By November 1971, some 
100,000 Bengalis could claim membership in these varied elements of the Mukti Bahini, and half of 
them were already operating inside East Pakistan.
