India struck back on the night of 10/11 December, taking a promontory above the Punch River 
opposite Kahuta in a well-conducted assault by 21 Punjab and 9 Rajputana Rifles.  Further south at 
Daruchian, however, a similar night attack by 14 Grenadiers was thrown back with heavy losses on
