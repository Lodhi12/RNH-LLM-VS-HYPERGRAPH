By 15 December, the Pakistani situation around Dacca was hopeless: the lone brigade of 36 
Division was broken, the newly arrived 314 Brigade was little more than a paper organization, 14 
Division was sitting demoralized and useless at Bhairab Bazar, and 39 Division had ceased to exist. 
On the morning of 16 December, Major General Mohammed Jamshed Khan drove out of Dacca to 
arrange the cease-fire.70
