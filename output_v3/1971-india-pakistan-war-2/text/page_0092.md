11  Sisson and Rose (187) note four elements in the Indian policy by late spring 1971: return of all refugees, transfer of 
power to a moderate Awami League administration in East Pakistan, use of force as necessary -- starting with support 
to the Mukti Bahini, but retaining the option to escalate to direct intervention -- and mobilization of international 
support.  For a recent Indian view of diplomacy during this period, see J. N. Dixit, Across Borders: Fifty Years of India’s 
Foreign Policy (New Delhi: Picus, 1998), 97-113.
