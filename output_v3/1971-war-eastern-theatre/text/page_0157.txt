Central Sector 
a. 
On 11 December, the Indians carried out a para-drop with 2 Para at 
Tangail.488 But, the troops of 101 Communication Zone could not progress 
ahead of Kasimpur till 14 December. The Central Sector was the only 
sector where our troops were able to slip out of Indian noose to fight them 
again north of Dacca. Commanding Officer 31 Baloch, Lieutenant Colonel 
Sultan Ahmed had reached Dacca after his escape from Jamalpur and 
was given the responsibility of checking the Indian along the Tungi Axis. 
He had about 100 men of his battalion with a few hundred others and 
some paramilitary troops. The Indian attack against 31 Baloch roadblock 
near Chandna was beaten back with the support of artillery fire.489
