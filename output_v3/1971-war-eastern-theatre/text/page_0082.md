b. 
Meanwhile, during the first week of November, the Indians attacked our 
positions east of River Tista. Bhurungamati was attacked and captured on 
14 November. Efforts to retake this position under the orders of Eastern 
Command failed and resulted in considerable losses to 25 Punjab.183 On 
night 20/21 November, enemy 4 Rajputs also crossed the border and 
attacked Nageshwari. The place was defended by a company of 8 Punjab 
and two companies of 25 Punjab. Enemy attack was repulsed. On night 
29/30 November enemy attacked the position for the second time. Own 
troops withheld the Indians that night but, withdrew from Nageshwari on 
30 November. Company of 8 Punjab joined 34 Punjab (R&S) elements at 
Thakurgaon; whereas, companies ex 25 Punjab withdrew to Saidpur.184 At 
the same time, Kurigram, Alipur and Lal Munir Hat also came under 
intense pressure and the area across River Tista became untenable. 
Around 30 November, troops east of Tista were pulled back destroying all 
bridges on the river.185 
c.
