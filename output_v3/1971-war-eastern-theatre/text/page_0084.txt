On 3 December, 71 Mountain Brigade was placed under 6 Mountain 
Division. On 6 December, at 2100 hours, the Indian 7 MLI tried to take 
Pirganj, but failed. A second attack came at 0800 hours on 9 December. 
Our troops destroyed four enemy tanks and forced the enemy to 
withdraw. 7 MLI suffered heavy causalities.20234 Punjab remained in 
possession of Pirganj.203  
k. 
Unable to take Pirganj, 7 MLI bypassed it and contacted Kanti Nagar 
Bridge on Dhepa River by approximately 2100 hours on 6 December. 
Here again Indians were greeted with a very heavy volume of fire. The 
bridge at Kanti Nagar had already been destroyed by own withdrawing 
troops. During the night, 7 MLI tried to get across the river, but failed due 
to strong opposition by elements of 34 Punjab.204
