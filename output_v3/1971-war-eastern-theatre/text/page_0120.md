After the capture of Kulaura, 6 Rajput commenced advance to 
Brahman Bazar on 6 December and occupied it without opposition. 
After some skirmishes enroute it pressed on toward Fenchuganj on    
7 December. Our opposition along this axis was weak, especially as 
22 Baloch was in no fit state to offer serious resistance.353 
n. 
Meanwhile 4 Corps stopped operations of 59 Mountain Brigade to 
Fenchuganj and ordered it to move to Kailashahar for subsequent 
employment in Brahmanbaria Sub-sector.  
o. 
On the morning of 7 December, 4 Corps shared intelligence with         
8 Mountain Division that there were indications that the 202-A and 313 
Brigades were withdrawing from Sylhet town and the Maulvi Bazar 
area to link up with their forces in the Ashuganj area. Most troops of 
our 202-A Brigade were spread out along the border north and east of 
Sylhet. Sylhet itself was reported to have few troops at this stage.354 
p. 
The Indians felt the situation was ripe for exploitation before we could 
recover and decide to hold Sylhet town in strength. It was proposed to 
heli-lift a battalion as near to Sylhet as possible to capture this 
important town before we could move sufficient forces to defend it.355 
Such an operation would also facilitate the advance of Echo Sector 
force along the Jaintiapur Axis for an early linkup with the heli-lifted 
force. It was appreciated that the linkup would be possible within
